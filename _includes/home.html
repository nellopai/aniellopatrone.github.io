About me

I am Lead AI Scientist at piur imaging in Vienna, Austria. I focus on creating AI solutions for medical applications and coordinate our AI development and infrastructure. The possibility to help others through my work is a strong motivator for me.

My background is in computer vision and deep learning. I have several years of experience deploying AI software, managing projects, and leading teams without formal hierarchy (lateral leadership).

I hold a PhD in computer science with a focus on computer vision, as well as an MSc and a BSc in computer science. I also have several years of professional research experience in mathematical image and data analysis. You can find my papers on the publications page and the full story in my biography.

Whatever the project, my goal is the same: to empower people with insights from data.
