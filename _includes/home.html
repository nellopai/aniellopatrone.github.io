About me

I am Lead AI Scientist at piur imaging in Vienna, Austria, where I lead a team of three AI engineers and set the AI roadmap in line with company goals. My work focuses on deep learning for medical applications, such as object tracking, thyroid and carotid segmentation, and federated learning. I also teach as a part-time lecturer at IMC University of Applied Sciences Krems.

My background is in computer vision: I hold a PhD in computer science with a focus on computer vision from the University of Vienna, as well as an MSc and a BSc in computer science. I am also a co-inventor on a patent application for anonymizing textual content in image training data.
