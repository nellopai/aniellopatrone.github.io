About me

I am Lead AI Scientist at piur imaging in Vienna, Austria, where I lead a team of three AI engineers and set the AI roadmap in line with company goals. My work focuses on deep learning for medical ultrasound: sensorless 3D tracking, segmentation of the thyroid, veins and carotid plaque, 3D thyroid volumetry, foundation models such as SAM and MedSAM, and federated learning.

The problem I find most interesting is sensorless 3D tracking: estimating how a hand-held ultrasound probe moves through space from the image stream itself, without relying on external tracking hardware. It is perception of motion in the physical world, on real clinical data and under real-world noise, and it is where my work comes closest to robotics and Physical AI: visual odometry, sensor fusion and getting learned models to behave reliably on real devices.

My path started in research. During my PhD in computer vision at the University of Vienna, I worked on mathematical models of visual attention in videos and on algorithms for image dejittering. I then moved to industry: first video-surveillance software at KiwiSecurity, then deep learning on mobile devices at Anyline, where I improved document scanning accuracy and speed by 20% and took on project management and Scrum Master responsibilities. Since 2021 I have been at piur imaging, first as Senior Deep Learning Scientist and, since 2023, as Lead. From 2024 to 2026 I also lectured at IMC University of Applied Sciences Krems.

I am also co-inventor of a patent on anonymizing textual content in image training data, granted in the US and in Europe. The details of each role are in my biography, and my papers are on the publications page.

Selected projects

  • Sensorless 3D tracking of ultrasound probes (piur imaging, R&D): deep learning models that estimate 3D rotations and translations of a hand-held probe from ultrasound images alone; evaluated on clinical data, the latest model reaches an error of 5% of the mean scan length across devices and organs.
  • Thyroid and carotid segmentation (piur imaging): production models for thyroid, vein and carotid plaque segmentation, and 3D thyroid volumetry with one-click segmentation in 20 s instead of 3 minutes of manual labelling.
  • Foundation models for medical imaging (piur imaging): fine-tuning large pretrained models such as SAM, MedSAM and Vision Transformers, including zero-shot segmentation of organs not seen in training.
  • Federated learning (piur imaging): a federated training setup with NVIDIA FLARE in a publicly funded (FFG) research project, so that models can learn from distributed data without pooling it in one place.
  • On-device deep learning for document scanning (Anyline): mobile models for document detection, sharpness and ID classification, shipped in the Anyline SDK; document detection improved scanning accuracy and speed by 20%.
  • AI development from scratch (piur imaging): the MLOps stack (Airflow, later ClearML), CI/CD and a standalone, tested research codebase that takes models from experiment to product.
